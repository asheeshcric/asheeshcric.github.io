About

I am a Research Scientist at Meta, where I work with the Facebook Navigation team on enhancing users' in-app navigation experience using Generative AI and machine learning. My work focuses on badging and notification signals that surface relevant content to users at scale.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the University of Texas at Arlington in 2023, specializing in machine learning, computer vision, and human-computer interaction. My research spans self-supervised learning, multi-modal fusion, and activity recognition from visual data.

Research Vision

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and human-computer interaction — with a focus on building systems that understand and respond to human behavior at scale. I am particularly interested in self-supervised and semi-supervised learning methods that can extract meaningful representations from large volumes of unlabeled multimodal data.

At Meta, I apply these foundations to real-world navigation challenges: using generative AI to understand user intent and surface relevant content across the Facebook app. I believe the most impactful ML systems are those that remain invisible to users — seamlessly improving their experience without demanding their attention.
