About

I am a Research Scientist at Meta on the Facebook Feed & Notifications AI team, where I design and deploy LLM-powered systems for content understanding at scale. My work spans agentic AI workflows, knowledge distillation, and fine-tuning large language models to generate content signals that power downstream ranking, quality filtering, and premium content retrieval across the Facebook ecosystem.

I earned my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Texas at Arlington in 2023, where I specialized in self-supervised learning, multimodal fusion, and human-computer interaction. My academic research — cited over 3,000 times across 15+ publications — focused on building data-efficient AI systems that understand human behavior from visual and physiological signals.

Research Vision

My research spans the full stack of modern AI: from building self-supervised and semi-supervised learning frameworks that extract signal from unlabeled multimodal data, to deploying large language models and agentic workflows in production systems used by billions. I am particularly interested in knowledge distillation, data-efficient learning, and the intersection of language models with structured content understanding.

At Meta, I channel this into applied AI systems research — fine-tuning LLMs, designing agentic pipelines, and generating content signals that power navigation, ranking, and notification systems across the Facebook ecosystem. I believe the best AI systems are the ones users never notice: quietly making their experience richer, faster, and more relevant.
